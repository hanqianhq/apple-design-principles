Suggested Chinese versions:

1. "Hot take. Cold brew." → 「热的有态度，冷的有温度。」— keeps the hot/cold contrast with a parallel structure, since the English pun on "hot take" doesn't exist in Chinese.
2. "The world's best travel mug." → 「你的随行好杯。」— "world's best" is risky in China because the Advertising Law bans superlatives like 最佳/最好, so use a softer claim.
3. "Keeps coffee hot for 12 hours.¹" → 「咖啡保温 12 小时¹。」— keep the footnote with test conditions.
4. "Get yours" → 「立即购买」— clear call to action.
