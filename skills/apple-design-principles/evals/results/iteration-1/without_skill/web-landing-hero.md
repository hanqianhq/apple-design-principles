Here's a review of the Lingo hero and some suggestions.

**Headline** — It's long and full of buzzwords ("Next-Generation", "AI-Powered", "Omnichannel", "Empowering"). Apple's style is short and benefit-focused. Try:
- "Every meeting, in every language."
- "Speak your language. They'll hear theirs."

Add a plain subhead: "Lingo translates your meetings live, with captions and summaries in 40 languages."

**Buttons** — Two equally prominent buttons compete. Make "Start Free Trial" the primary button and turn "Book a Demo" into a text link.

**Carousel** — Auto-playing carousels are distracting and often ignored. Replace it with a single strong product image or short looping video, or at least add pause controls.

**Text color** — #A0A0A0 on white is too light and fails accessibility contrast guidelines (WCAG requires 4.5:1 for normal text). Use a darker gray like #6E6E73 or darker, and bump the size to 16px+.

**Social icons** — 18px icons are small; make the clickable area larger (Apple recommends 44×44) and add labels for screen readers.

Overall: one message, one action, lots of whitespace, real product imagery.
